## Corrections and discrepancies

- **R11** — Was 17:00–23:00 expected complete $298.70. Hours after 9 pm Wednesday are outside the Retail span (Table 2) and are overtime for full-time staff unless trading hours extend it (cl 15.2), so that shift cannot be complete; moved to R11b.
- **R15** — Was expected complete. Without pay period dates the week's other hours are unknown, so weekly overtime (Table 10) can't be ruled out.
- **R20** — Was expected complete with no comparison. Owner rule: any gross-only comparison is Partial.
- **R26** — Was expected partial when the list stopped at 2025; 2026 calendars from each state government were added.
- **Retail casual overtime** — earlier text said it was absent from the award. It exists (cl 21.2 Table 11 column 3: 175% / 225% / 225% / 275%); only the Fair Work pay database lacks records, so it stays Not assessed.
- **Hospitality penalty table** — previously cited as Table 12; it is cl 29.2 Table 14. Midnight–7am addition (record 11954, $4.42 from 2026-07-01) matches the award text.
- **Breaks** — unspecified breaks were previously taken from the highest-paid minutes and reported Complete. Now only allowed when every minute pays the same; otherwise Partial with a question.
- **Overtime** — a full set of shifts no longer counts as proof of no overtime: full-time and Hospitality/Fast Food part-time need roster confirmation, Retail/Fast Food part-time need guaranteed hours, and weekly checks need the pay period and every shift.
- **Pay comparison** — gross-only is always Partial; pay lines need confirmation they cover all wages; lines exceeding gross or a casual loading on a non-casual employee are Partial.
- **Public holidays** — added 2026 calendars from all eight state/territory governments; NSW Bank Holiday and TAS Easter Tuesday marked restricted; Melbourne Cup, Brisbane show and similar marked regional (needs confirmation); Christmas/New Year's Eve part-days in SA, NT and QLD; VIC AFL Grand Final 2025 added. WA King's Birthday is marked regional because some areas observe a different date.
